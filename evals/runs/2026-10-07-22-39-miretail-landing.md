by hand. What it could not do or found confusing, and what changed in the CLI:

  answered `E_TARGET_EXISTS`). The blog now starts empty, and `add blog-post --replace` writes a post again.

- `add page --nav header` could only append: `--nav-before <id>` places the entry.

  to pass `--type`, and `brand --help` prints its flags.
- A failed `--dry-run` reported `"dryRun": false` in its error envelope: it now echoes the flag.
- The evaluator read the `>` of a read-only Python heredoc as a redirect: heredoc bodies and quoted strings are no
  longer scanned.
