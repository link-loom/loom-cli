# Mi Retail landing

Build the public website of **Mi Retail**, the operations center for your business: everything an organization
runs, in one place, with the work every person has to move today.

- Product name: **Mi Retail**. Address: **miretail.com.co**. In one sentence: "The operations center for your
  business: everything your organization runs, in one place." In Spanish: "El centro de operaciones de tu negocio:
  todo lo que tu organización opera, en un solo lugar."
- The site is in English and Spanish and opens in English.
- Brand: indigo #3c4876 with cyan #54c5eb, a coral accent #f0655c, and near-black buttons.
- People sign in at https://app.miretail.com.co/login and sign up at https://app.miretail.com.co/signup.
- Sales write to sales@miretail.com.co and support to support@miretail.com.co. Mi Retail is a Blackwood Stone
  Holdings, Inc. company (https://blackwoodstoneholdings.com), credited in the footer.
- The home opens with "The operations center your **whole business** runs on." ("El centro de operaciones con el
  que opera **todo tu negocio**.", the bold words highlighted). Below it, a dark band about governance with three
  points: every action has an author, every change leaves evidence, every decision keeps its reason. Then a few
  frequent questions, and the closing call to action.
- A Pricing page in the main menu, and a Company page in the footer.
- A blog with the categories product, operations and guides, and a first post: "Why one board beats ten tools"
  ("Por qué un tablero le gana a diez herramientas").
- Search across the site.

When you are done the project must pass its own verification.
