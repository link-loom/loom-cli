# Mi Retail workspace

Build the web workspace of **Mi Retail**: the operations center a retail business runs from, for the people who run
the operation (not for the people who administer the platform).

- Product name: **Mi Retail**. In one sentence: "The operations center every role runs the organization from."
- People sign in with Veripass, and new organizations can sign up.
- It belongs to the StoneOS ecosystem: the StoneOS launchpad, the apps and the App Store are there, and so is the
  Sommatic Command Center.
- Advanced settings shows every platform of the ecosystem.
- The interface opens in Spanish.
- Brand: keep Mi Retail's colours. Header #3c4876, its logo area #2f3a5f, primary colour #3c4876, page background
  #eff3f9.
- The team works with **projects** (in the workspace domain of the backend): each one has a name (required), a long
  description and a status, active or archived. People need a standard list with a quick view, opening a project in a
  new tab and copying its link. Projects have a row in the sidebar and a shortcut in the "+" menu. In Spanish they are
  "Proyecto" / "Proyectos".

When you are done the project must pass its own verification.
