# ÊTRUNE ID admin

Build **ÊTRUNE ID**, the administration console of ÊTRUNE's identity platform, for the people who administer the
platform (not for the people who use it day to day).

- Product name: **ÊTRUNE ID**. In one sentence: "Identity, access and trust for every ÊTRUNE customer."
- People sign in with Veripass. Nobody signs up here: accounts are created by the administrators.
- It is not part of StoneOS and has no Command Center.
- The interface opens in Spanish.
- Brand: ÊTRUNE's dark coffee. Header #563e2e and primary colour #563e2e; the logo area a touch darker than the header.
- The administrators manage **customers** (in the identity domain of the backend): each one has a name (required), an
  email and a status, active or suspended. A compact list is enough, with a quick view and a confirmation before
  deleting one. In Spanish they are "Cliente" / "Clientes".

When you are done the project must pass its own verification.
